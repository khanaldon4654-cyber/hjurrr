Mero pyaro Dalli 🥹❤️🫂

K tha, hamro friendship mero life ko sabai bhanda ramro memories madhye euta bannechha. Timisanga kura garda man halka hunchha, timro smile dekhda khusi lagchha, ani timi risayeko bela pani timilai manauna man lagchha. 😭💗

Jindagima dherai manche aaulan, dherai manche jaalan, tara timijasto special manche sadhai mero life ma rahirahos bhanne chahana chha. 🫶🏻❤️ Hamro bichma kahile distance aayo, kura kam bhayo, ya misunderstanding bhayo bhane pani hamro friendship ko value kahile kam nahos.

Ma sadhai perfect friend nahola, kahile galti pani hola, tara timilai hurt garne intention kahile hune chhaina. 🥺🫂 Timi dukhi huda malai samjhinnu, khusi huda malai share garnu, ani life le garo banayeko bela timi eklai chhau bhanne feel kahile nahos.

Dalli, hamro friendship temporary haina, lifetime ko mitho bond banos. ❤️♾️ Samay badliyos, thau badliyos, life badliyos, tara hamro friendship ko maya ra respect kahile nabadliyos. Thank you for being such a beautiful part of my life. Love you as a best friend, mero Dalli! 🥹💗🫂

— Timro sathi, Amrit 💙
